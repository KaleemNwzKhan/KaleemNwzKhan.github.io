Kaleem Nawaz Khan

Rochester, NY 14623

Enabling autonomous vehicles to accurately perceive beyond line-of-sight.

I am a Ph.D. student candidate (View CV) in the CS Department at Rochester Institute of Technology, where I work with Fawad Ahmad in the Networked Sensing Systems Lab.

Research. My research focuses on collaborative perception between vehicles and roadside infrastructure. It enables sensing beyond line-of-sight. I study the co-design of perception, communication, and computation. The goal is reliable and safe autonomous decision-making. My work has appeared in top mobile and sensing systems venues, including MobiSys and SenSys.

Background. I received B.S. (2015) and M.S. (2018) degrees in CS with honors from the NUCES , Pakistan. Before starting Ph.D., I was a Research Team Lead at the Intelligent Information Processing Lab under the National Center for Artificial Intelligence (NCAI) and served as a Lecturer at Bahria University Islamabad and the University of Engineering and Technology, Mardan.
